Our Mission

The Lantern Guild is an alliance of independent developers who create free tools that help people recognize scams, evaluate what they read, and use technology with confidence.

We believe everyone deserves to understand the digital world we inhabit, regardless of their technical skill or what they can pay.

By using Lantern Guild tools, people benefit from:

  • The ability to spot deception before it costs them money, privacy, or trust
  • Better judgment about what information to believe and act on
  • Practical knowledge to navigate everyday technology without fear or confusion
  • Tools and knowledge to safeguard their privacy and security
  • The confidence to help others do the same

Our commitment to neutrality:

We do not attempt to change anyone's religious views, political beliefs, or personal values. Our tools deal solely in facts and evidence. We help people recognize misinformation, scams, and security threats—not persuade them toward any ideology or worldview.

We build in the open, share what we learn, and welcome any developer who commits to these values and creates tools that serve these goals.
